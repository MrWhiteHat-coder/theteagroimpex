01About Us

From Indian soil to global tables.

Thete Agro Impex is an agricultural products exporter based in Nashik, Maharashtra — one of India's most productive farming regions. We connect Indian growers with international buyers who need dependable supply, consistent grading and honest communication.

We work directly with farms, mills and processors, and stay involved from the first enquiry to the final dispatch — so buyers always know where their goods come from and who to talk to.
